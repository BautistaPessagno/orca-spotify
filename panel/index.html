Connect Spotify

Now Playing controls Spotify through the Spotify Web API, on any device you play on.

  1. Have Spotify Premium.Spotify only lets Premium accounts control playback.
  2. Create an app on the Spotify dashboard. Pick “Web API” and accept the Developer Terms.
  3. Add this redirect URI to the app.
  4. Paste the app’s Client ID.
  5. Connect, and approve in the browser. Orca asks once whether Now Playing may open accounts.spotify.com.
  6. Open Spotify on any device.The player shows up here.
Orca can’t reach a keyring on this computer, so the login lasts only until Now Playing’s worker stops (5 minutes idle). Install gnome-keyring or KWallet to keep it.
